A practical approach to AI

Use new tools where they genuinely help.

AI is not the answer to every business challenge. Start with a clear task, consider the risks, and choose tools that support people rather than add extra complexity.

Potential areas to explore

Small, considered improvements.

Begin with a real need in your workflow and evaluate whether an AI-supported approach is useful, safe, and appropriate.

Organise information

Help teams sort and summarise routine information while keeping people responsible for important decisions.

Support communication

Explore drafting support for common messages, with a person reviewing details and tone before anything is sent.

Review workflows

Look for repetitive steps that could be improved, and compare AI options with simpler automation first.

Start with the right questions

Useful AI needs sensible safeguards.

Protect information

Understand what data a tool receives and avoid sharing personal or confidential details without approval.

Check the output

AI-generated content can be incomplete or incorrect. Review it before relying on or sharing it.

Keep people involved

Choose processes where staff can question results and make the final call when it matters.
